# Surface brief: the World Cup 2026 page (site/world-cup-2026.html, once site/index.html)

Scope: the whole page, shell included: site masthead, hero, the eight chapters, footer. The Euro 2028, Copa América 2028 and Thiago placeholder pages inherit the shell. Visitor mode: Read.

Audience and job: portfolio visitors judging craft and thinking, football fans and data readers second. They come to understand what was simulated and how closely it matched the real 2026 World Cup. Proof: the runs themselves, read from site/data/wc2026. Constraints: plain HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript, no motion library, no build step; every number from the JSON; the pinned green-and-paper palette; light theme; prefers-reduced-motion honoured; the copy and the eight chapters in their order stay, sentences may be tightened.

Chosen direction and memorable moment: the tournament wall chart; the pinned hero whose champion column fills as you scroll and holds the page until the last number lands.

Unresolved: the hero column's ink floor (55%) is provisional until seen on localhost; chapters two to eight vary the motion thesis with their own content, decided at each section checkpoint.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The tournament as a newspaper wall chart filled in 100,000 times: one printed sheet on which ink and count are the whole story. It refuses the heat-shaded probability dashboard and the cream, italic-serif, mono-kicker editorial scroll the site has today.

OWN-WORLD: Paper #F2F6F1, ink #10261A, orange #C2410C only for the real result and the hot number, the pinned nine-step fate scale as cell colour whose strength follows the share, numbers always in full ink. League Gothic capitals for mastheads and chapter titles, Old Standard TT for reading, News Cycle for every table and label. Broadsheet rules: hairlines between rows, 3px rules under mastheads and around chapter titles, which sit centred between two of them. No kickers, no chapter numbers, no cards, no shadows, no glow.

STORY: The visitor reads down one sheet. The hero states the claim and its champion column proves it; each chapter is another region of the same chart; they leave knowing the model, its result and how close it came to reality.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Site masthead (wordmark left, tournaments and links right, a Chapters link, no rail), the chart masthead under a 3px rule, the first-person headline in League Gothic left over the lede in Old Standard TT, the champion column right (twelve teams and the rest, blank until scrolled), and a 3px rule at the bottom edge as the limit. The hero is pinned: one screen of scroll fills the column top to bottom, the last row finishing last; it holds a beat; then the page releases into chapter one, which is absent until then.

FORM: The tournament wall chart, candidate 1 of 7 on the grounded list, chosen as IMPECCABLE'S PICK over the assigned roll (candidate 4, the results board); seed key bed5693b; code-led build.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Signature interaction and motion grammar

The sheet is filled in as you read it: ink prints and counts count up; nothing slides, rises or fades in from below. Progress follows the scroll on the way down and never un-prints on the way up. The one authored moment is the pinned hero. Each chapter varies the thesis with its own content: chapter one's rows print as they cross the reading line, the bracket draws its most likely path, the picker reprints its plates. Feedback: a hovered row darkens its ink. Reduced motion: the finished sheet, no pin, no count-ups.
