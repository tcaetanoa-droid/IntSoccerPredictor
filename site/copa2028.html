Copa América 2028

Coming after the draw.
Status
Host, dates and format not yet announced. The 2024 edition had 16 teams in four groups; the file is ready for whatever 2028 turns out to be.

What will be here

The same eight chapters as the World Cup page, from the ratings the teams carry into the tournament: who wins it, every group, the most likely bracket, the hosts, the underdogs, the paradoxes, a page per team, and the maths. The simulations run the day after the finals draw and are scored against the real tournament afterwards.

Model change

The Euro 2028 and Copa América 2028 runs add the Dixon–Coles correction for low-scoring draws, the one weakness the World Cup calibration showed.

IntSoccerPredictor

100,000 replays of international tournaments from Elo ratings. A personal project by Thiago Caetano, not affiliated with FIFA, UEFA, CONMEBOL or eloratings.net.

Data

Ratings and match histories from eloratings.net. Tournament regulations from FIFA, as documented on Wikipedia. Flags from flagcdn.
