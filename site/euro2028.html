Euro 2028

Coming after the draw.
Dates
9 June to 9 July 2028
Hosts
England, Scotland, Wales, Republic of Ireland
Format
24 teams, six groups of four, the four best third-placed teams go through, no third-place match
Status
Qualifying draw on 6 December 2026. The finals draw has not been held.

What will be here

The same eight chapters as the World Cup page, from the ratings the 24 teams carry into the tournament: who wins it, every group, the most likely bracket, the hosts, the underdogs, the paradoxes, a page per team, and the maths. The simulations run the day after the finals draw and are scored against the real tournament afterwards.

Model change

The Euro 2028 and Copa América 2028 runs add the Dixon–Coles correction for low-scoring draws, the one weakness the World Cup calibration showed.

IntSoccerPredictor

100,000 replays of international tournaments from Elo ratings. A personal project by Thiago Caetano, not affiliated with FIFA, UEFA, CONMEBOL or eloratings.net.

Data

Ratings and match histories from eloratings.net. Tournament regulations from FIFA, as documented on Wikipedia. Flags from flagcdn.
